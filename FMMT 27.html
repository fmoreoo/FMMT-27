Football Manager Moneyball Tool

Load your save from FM27

Have FM27 open with your save loaded, then click Load from game.

Data is read from the game on this computer only. Nothing is uploaded.

Inspired by and built on the FM24 Statistics Spreadsheet by Fmplaya, the source of its stat list and league ratings. Ability weights from fm-scoring by Jamie Bolden (MIT). Free software under the GNU GPL v3. Not affiliated with Sports Interactive or SEGA.

Squad

Shortlist

Add players with the ☆ next to their name in World Scout, or from their profile. The shortlist is kept between sessions. Players are stored by their game ID, so it carries over when you reload from the game.

Compare

Percentiles (0-99) are against the percentile pool, in the leagues chosen under “Percentiles against” in Player search (all leagues by default). Players below the pool's minimum minutes show “–” and an empty chart.

Players

Every loaded player is listed. Percentiles (0-99) are against the percentile pool: players with at least the pool's minimum minutes (300 unless changed in Player search). Players below it are still placed within the pool but sorted after pool players; players with no minutes or incomplete stats show “–” and sort to the bottom. “Defensive actions” is approximated as tackles won + interceptions + blocks + clearances. Market value is FM’s estimate range, sorted by its lower end. Average rating, market value and age come straight from the game; positions list every position rated Accomplished (15+) or better. ☆ adds a player to your shortlist (My Club).

Compare players

Charts show percentiles (0-99) against every loaded player who meets the minutes limit set in Scout. For fouls, cards, offsides and goals conceded, lower is better, so the percentile is flipped.
